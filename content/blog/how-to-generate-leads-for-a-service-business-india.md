Search "lead generation India" and you get two things: companies selling lists of phone numbers, and platforms selling pay-per-lead subscriptions. Both produce the same result for a one-person service business. You spend money, call strangers who did not ask to hear from you, and close almost nobody.

A lead is a person who has a problem you solve and has shown it. That signal is the whole point. Lists do not have it. Here is where leads with the signal come from, what each source costs, and the one thing that decides whether any of them turn into clients.

## The one thing first: a lead needs somewhere to land

Every lead source below sends a person to the same place: one page with who you help, the offer with a price, one proof point, and a way to book. If that page does not exist, leads leak. If it has no price, half of them book a call to ask the price and never buy.

Build [the one page](/blog/do-you-need-a-website-to-start-a-business) before you touch any of the sources below. Then, and only then, worry about volume.

## Source 1: Referrals, deliberately asked for

Referrals are the highest-converting lead source in India by a wide margin, and almost nobody asks for them systematically. Three habits:

- **The three-line message** to five people you know each week, asking who they know with the problem. It is [the fastest first-client method](/blog/how-to-get-your-first-client) and it keeps working after the first client.
- **The ninety-day check-in.** Three months after a project ends, message the client with one useful observation about their business and no ask. Half of these turn into work or referrals within the year.
- **The ask at the finish.** When a client is happy at the end of a project, say: "Who else do you know who's dealing with this? I'd rather work with people you send than with strangers." Then stop talking.

Cost: nothing. Time: an hour a week. Expect one to two qualified leads a week within a month.

## Source 2: Google Business Profile, if you serve a place

If any of your customers are local, clinics, shops, schools, restaurants, a complete Google Business Profile sends phone calls before your website is ever opened. It is free, it takes an hour to set up properly, and most one-person businesses in India either have not claimed it or have left it half empty.

Fill every field. Add photos of you and your work. Ask every client for a review, with a direct link. Post once a fortnight. Reply to every review. In tier-two and tier-three cities, this alone can be the main lead source for a local service.

## Source 3: Search, for the questions your customers type

People type their problems into Google at eleven at night. "Why is my clinic not getting patients from the website." "How much does a CA charge for GST filing for a small shop." Each of those is a lead that has raised its hand. Write the answer, one question per page, and put your offer at the end.

This is slow, three to six months to start, and then it is the most reliable source you have, because it keeps sending leads while you deliver client work. [How to increase website traffic without paid ads](/blog/how-to-increase-website-traffic-without-paid-ads) covers the method.

## Source 4: One platform, proof posts and messages

LinkedIn for B2B and consulting. Instagram for consumer services. Two proof posts a week, fifteen minutes a day of comments on your customers' posts, and ten direct messages a week. Three hours total. The [LinkedIn method](/blog/how-to-get-clients-from-linkedin-as-a-consultant) has the schedule and the message templates.

## Source 5: A lead magnet, once the other four are running

A lead magnet is something useful you give away in exchange for a name and a number: a checklist, a scorecard, a short guide. It turns visitors who are not ready to book into people you can follow up with. It is powerful, and it is also the source most people build first, which is backwards. A lead magnet with no traffic collects nothing. Build it in month two or three, once sources one to four are sending people. [What is a lead magnet and how to create one](/blog/what-is-a-lead-magnet) explains what makes one work.

## What each source costs and yields

| Source | Cost | Time to first lead | Leads a month, once running | Quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Referrals, asked for | ₹0 | Days | 4 to 8 | Highest |
| Google Business Profile | ₹0 | Weeks | 5 to 20, local | High |
| Search articles | ₹0 | 3 to 6 months | 10 to 30 | High |
| LinkedIn or Instagram | ₹0 | Weeks | 4 to 10 | Medium to high |
| Lead magnet | ₹0 | Needs traffic first | Depends on traffic | Medium |
| Bought lists | ₹5,000 to ₹50,000 | Immediate | Many | Very low |
| Pay-per-lead platforms | ₹500 to ₹3,000 per lead | Immediate | Many | Low, shared with competitors |

The bought options are last on purpose. They produce the appearance of activity, and they teach you to sell to people who do not want to be sold to, which is the wrong skill to practise.

## Qualifying: not every lead deserves a call

Once leads arrive, protect your calendar. A short form before the booking link with three questions does it: what do they need, when do they need it by, and are they in a position to invest. The people who answer "just exploring" get a useful email, not a slot. This is exactly how [the Launch Call application](/call) on this site works, and it is why the calls that happen are worth having.

## The order to build it

1. **Week 1:** the page, with the price on it.
2. **Weeks 1 to 4:** referrals, five messages a week. Google Business Profile if you are local.
3. **Weeks 3 onward:** one search article a fortnight.
4. **Week 5 onward:** one platform, three hours a week.
5. **Month 3:** the lead magnet, and a simple follow-up sequence behind it.

Do not skip to step five. It is the most common mistake in Indian small-business marketing, and it is why so many people have an ebook and no clients.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest way to generate leads for a small business in India?

Asking for referrals with a specific message. It costs nothing, works within days, and produces the highest-quality leads of any source. Most people never do it because it feels like asking for a favour. It is asking for an introduction, which people are happy to give.

### Do lead generation companies in India work for consultants?

Rarely, for a one-person consultancy. They sell volume, and volume of the wrong person is worse than nothing because it fills your calendar with calls that go nowhere. They can work for high-volume local services with a sales team. For consulting, build the five sources above.

### How many leads do I need a month?

Fewer than you think. For a consultant selling ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000 engagements, ten qualified leads a month, three or four calls, and one or two clients is a healthy business. Work backward from the number of clients you can actually deliver for.

### Which stage of the journey is lead generation?

Stage five, Launch. If you are generating leads but not closing them, the problem is usually stage three, the offer. The [Fit Score](/fit) sorts out which one is yours.
