Search this question and you get registration guides: pick a structure, get a PAN, open a current account, register for GST. All true, all useful, and all the wrong first step. Registration is what you do once someone has agreed to pay you. Before that, you have a different problem: what exactly are you selling, and to whom?

I have run digital businesses from a desk at home for twenty years and built websites for more than 2,500 businesses, most of them at the exact moment they were starting. This is the order that works, from home, with a laptop, in India, in 2026.

## Step 1: Pick a customer, not an idea

Ideas are cheap and abstract. A person who has already asked you for help is a business waiting to be named.

Write down three people who asked you for help with your expertise in the last twelve months. Real names. The colleague who wanted to know how you structured the vendor contract. The friend's father with the export business who asked about GST on services. The founder who asked you to look at her hiring plan. One of those three is your first customer, and their problem, in their words, is your business.

This is what most guides skip, and it is why so many consulting businesses in India stall at "I have a website but nobody calls". You cannot market to "businesses". You can market to Dr. Mehta, who lost two patients last month because his clinic has no online presence.

## Step 2: Choose your model

A consultant tells people what to do. That is one of three ways to sell the same expertise. You can also do it for them (Services) or teach them to do it (Coaching). For the customer from step one, ask: do they want it done, decided, or taught?

If they want it decided, you are a consultant, and this guide is for you. If they want it done, you are a Services business and the same steps apply with a different offer. The full comparison is in [consulting vs coaching vs services](/blog/consulting-vs-coaching-vs-services).

## Step 3: Package one offer

An offer has four parts: the result, for whom, at a price, in a timeframe. "Business consulting" is a skill. "A 90-day pricing overhaul for a D2C brand under ₹1 crore, ₹60,000, with a written plan in week two" is an offer.

The consulting-specific rule: attach the offer to a decision or a number. "I'll help you grow" is not buyable. "I'll tell you which two of your six products to kill, and what to do with the money" is. Consulting sells when the client can picture the decision they will be able to make afterwards.

For how to set the number, read [how to price consulting services in India](/blog/how-to-price-consulting-services-india). Short version: price the value of the decision, not your hours.

## Step 4: One page, not a brand

You do not need a logo suite, a twelve-page website, or a company name with "Solutions" in it. You need one page with four things on it: who you help, the offer in one sentence with the price, one proof point, and a way to book you.

After 2,500 websites, the pattern is clear. The people who get a client in a month put up one page in a week. The people still "working on the site" a year later started with a brand. [Do you need a website to start a business](/blog/do-you-need-a-website-to-start-a-business) goes into exactly what belongs on that one page.

## Step 5: Get the first client from people you know

Your first consulting client will come from your network, not from ads. Send this to five people today:

> Hi [name]. I've started helping [who you help] with [the result]. Who do you know who's dealing with that right now?

It asks for a referral, not a sale, so people answer it. The full method, including what to say when someone replies, is in [how to get your first client without ads](/blog/how-to-get-your-first-client).

## Step 6: Now register

Once someone has agreed to pay you, or has actually paid you, set up the boring parts. In India, in order of urgency:

- **Sole proprietorship** is enough to start. Your home address is your business address. No registration is required until turnover crosses the GST threshold, which is ₹20 lakh a year for services in most states.
- **A current account** in your name or trade name, so client payments do not mix with personal money. Most banks open one for a proprietor with PAN, Aadhaar and a utility bill.
- **GST registration** when you cross ₹20 lakh, or earlier if a client insists on a GST invoice. Many corporate clients do, so registering early is often worth it.
- **An invoice template** with your PAN on it, sent the day the client says yes, half up front.
- **Udyam registration** is free and takes ten minutes. It helps with some client vendor forms.

None of this needs a lawyer for a one-person consultancy. Move to a private limited company only when a client requires it or when you are hiring.

## Step 7: Systemize before you scale

Once you have two or three clients, three boring things keep the business alive: a proposal template you fill in rather than rewrite, one calendar link so nobody emails to find a time, and one invoice flow. Set them up once. Then, and only then, think about raising prices, selling a second offer to existing clients, and eventually a helper.

## What it costs to start

Honest numbers for a home-based consultancy in India:

| Item | Cost | Needed before first client? |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop, internet, phone | Already have | Yes |
| One-page website on a domain | ₹2,000 to ₹6,000 a year | Helpful, not required |

The real cost is time: five to ten hours a week for ninety days. That is the pace at which stages one to five fit for most people with a real skill.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I start a consulting business while employed in India?

Usually yes, if your employment contract does not prohibit it and you are not consulting for competitors. Many consultants run the first year alongside a job and leave when the business can carry them. Check your contract's exclusivity clause, and keep the two strictly separate.

### Do I need experience to become a consultant?

You need experience in the thing you consult on, not in consulting. Ten years of running operations makes you an operations consultant the day someone pays you for advice. What you do not need is a certification.

### How much can a home-based consultant earn in India?

It depends entirely on the offer. Consultants who sell decisions with a number attached charge ₹40,000 to ₹2,00,000 per engagement even at the start. Consultants who sell "advice by the hour" get ₹1,500 an hour and a cap. Package the offer first.
