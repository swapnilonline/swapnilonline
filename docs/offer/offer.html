Straight answers

Questions people ask before they say yes

Is a 6-figure recurring income guaranteed?

No. Anyone who guarantees income is lying to you. It is the number we build toward, and the maths is done for your case on the call: your price × your clients × your hours. What is guaranteed is the first paying client, or we keep working.

I have a full-time job.

Fine, if you have five to ten hours a week. The Challenge needs 45 minutes with me and about four hours of doing. Many people build while employed and leave when the business can carry them.

Is this a course?

No. There are no videos. Every week we sit down 1:1 and work on your business. You leave each session with something finished, not notes.

What if I am afraid nobody will buy?

Everyone is. You say your price to me first, in practice. Then to a client. The fear does not survive the first yes, and if there is no yes by day 90, we keep going.

Why is there a Fit Score and a call first?

I work with around ten Challenge clients at a time and take six free calls a week. The score and the call make sure the ninety days will work for both of us. If not yet, I will say so.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The Challenge is three payments of ₹27,500. The Foundation is one payment of ₹7,500, credited in full if you join the Challenge within 60 days.
