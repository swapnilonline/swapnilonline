The 7-Day Digital Business Foundation

Your seven-day plan.

Day 1 of 7 · ONE PERSON

Name your customer. Choose your model.

Today's goal. By tonight you can say one real person's name, the situation they are in, and whether you will do it for them, decide it for them, or package it for them.

On the call with Swapnil
  • 90-minute call, 1:1
  • Your skill: what people pay you for, or ask you for
  • The three people who asked you for help this year
  • Pick one. Describe their situation in their words
  • Choose: services, consulting or products, and why
Your task today
  1. Write the names of three real people who asked you for help in the last twelve months.
  2. Circle one. Write one line about the situation they were in when they asked.
  3. Write your model in one word: services, consulting, or products.
  4. Send me the page by 6pm.
Worksheet
Three people who asked me for help
The one I am starting with, and their situation, in their words
My model for the next 90 days, and why
Swapnil checks
  • The person is specific: a type in a situation, not a category
  • Someone real has asked, with a name
  • One model, not two
  • The model matches how they want to be helped: done, decided, or taught
Done when

You can say one person's name and describe their problem in a sentence they would nod at.

Day 2 of 7 · ONE PROBLEM

Price the problem.

Today's goal. By tonight you know what this problem costs your person, in money or time, per month. That number becomes your pricing power.

On the call with Swapnil
  • 20-minute check-in
  • What happens to your person when this stays unsolved
  • What it costs them: money lost, hours lost, sleep lost
  • The expensive version of the problem, if today's version is a nice-to-have
Your task today
  1. Message one or two people who have this problem. Ask: what does it cost you each month when this is not fixed?
  2. Write their answers in their words.
  3. Put a number on it, even a rough one.
  4. Send me the page by 6pm.
Worksheet
What it costs them when it stays unsolved, in their words
The number: per month, in money or hours
Who loses the most when this is unsolved
Swapnil checks
  • The cost is stated as a number, not a feeling
  • The words came from a real person, not from the client's guess
  • The problem is urgent and expensive, not a nice-to-have
Done when

You can say what the problem costs, and your person would agree with the number.

Day 3 of 7 · ONE PROMISE

Say your offer in one sentence. Then say the price out loud.

Today's goal. By tonight your offer exists as one sentence: the result, for whom, at a price, in a timeframe. And you have said the price to me without flinching.

On the call with Swapnil
  • 30-minute call
  • Fill the four blanks together: [result] for [person] at [price] in [timeframe]
  • Price from the number on day 2: a fraction of what the problem costs
  • Say it out loud, three times, until it sounds decided
Your task today
  1. Write the sentence. Ugly is fine. Complete is what matters.
  2. Say it to one friend. Ask them to repeat it back, price included.
  3. If they cannot, change the words, not the price.
  4. Send me the sentence and what your friend said, by 6pm.
Worksheet
The result
For whom
The price
The timeframe
The offer, in one sentence
What my friend repeated back
Swapnil checks
  • All four parts present
  • Price is a fraction of the day-2 number, and above ₹1,500 an hour when divided by the hours
  • A number that sounds decided: ₹45,000, not ₹43,750
  • Client has said the price out loud, to me and to one other person
Done when

A friend can repeat your offer back to you, including the price, after hearing it once.

Day 4 of 7 · PROOF

Turn what you have done into one line of proof.

Today's goal. By tonight you have one proof point in writing: a result you got for someone, a review, or a before-and-after. If you have none, you know exactly which small job produces one this month.

On the call with Swapnil
  • 20-minute check-in
  • Everything you have done for anyone in this area, paid or unpaid
  • Pick the one with a visible result
  • Turn it into one line: who, what changed, in what time
  • If there is nothing yet: design the small job that produces it
Your task today
  1. List every time you helped someone with this, even informally.
  2. For the best one: message that person and ask for one line you can quote.
  3. Write the proof line.
  4. No proof yet? Write down who you will do a small paid version for this month, and at what price.
Worksheet
Times I have helped someone with this
The one I will use, and what changed for them
My proof line, or the small job that will produce it
Swapnil checks
  • One proof point, specific, with a result
  • Real words from a real person where possible
  • If none: a named person, a price and a date for the small job
Done when

You have one line you would be comfortable putting on your page under the offer.

Day 5 of 7 · ONE PAGE

Plan the page. Write the first five messages.

Today's goal. By tonight the one page that books clients exists in a document, and the five messages that will bring your first conversations are written.

On the call with Swapnil
  • 30-minute call
  • The four things on the page: who you help, the offer with the price, one proof, one way to book
  • Draft the headline and the offer line together
  • The three-line message, filled in with your blanks
  • Choose the five people
Your task today
  1. Write the page in a document: headline, offer line, proof line, how to book.
  2. Fill in the three-line message: Hi [name]. I've started helping [who] with [result]. Who do you know who's dealing with that right now?
  3. Write the five names you will send it to on day 6.
  4. Send me the document by 6pm.
Worksheet
Headline: who I help and the result
The offer line, with the price
The proof line
How people book me: calendar link or WhatsApp
The three-line message, filled in
The five people
Swapnil checks
  • Page has exactly four things, in order, nothing else
  • Price is on the page
  • Booking is a link or a button, not a contact form
  • Five people are named and reachable
Done when

A stranger reading the page for eight seconds would know who it is for, what it costs, and how to book.

Day 6 of 7 · PIPELINE

Send the five messages. Map the next ten conversations.

Today's goal. By tonight the five messages are sent, and you know where the next ten conversations will come from.

On the call with Swapnil
  • 20-minute check-in, after the messages are sent
  • What happened with each of the five
  • What to say to each kind of reply: a name, a maybe, a silence
  • Where the next ten come from: past clients, ex-colleagues, groups, one platform
Your task today
  1. Send all five messages before noon. Not this week. Today.
  2. Log each reply in the table below.
  3. Reply to anyone who names someone: ask for an introduction.
  4. List ten more people or places for next week.
Worksheet
Sent to / reply / next step (five rows)
The next ten: people or places
Swapnil checks
  • All five sent, today, in the client's own words
  • Replies answered the same day
  • Introductions asked for, not just thanked
  • Next ten are specific, not 'LinkedIn'
Done when

Five messages sent, every reply answered, and ten more names on the list.

Day 7 of 7 · THE NUMBER

Your recurring-income plan. Then the decision.

Today's goal. By tonight you have the maths on one page, price × clients × hours, the road from here to a 6-figure month, and a decision: launch alone, or build it together.

On the call with Swapnil
  • 45-minute call
  • Price × clients × hours, with your numbers
  • The hours check: does it fit the time you actually have
  • The road: what happens in weeks 2 to 12 if we build together
  • The honest decision, either way
Your task today
  1. Fill in the formula with your real numbers.
  2. Check the hours. If they do not fit, raise the price, not the hours.
  3. Write the date you will have your first paying client, alone or with me.
  4. Tell me your decision on the call, not by message.
Worksheet
Price per client, per month
Clients I can serve at once
Hours per client, per week
Every month =
Hours check: clients × hours per client, against the hours I have
The date of my first paying client
My decision
Swapnil checks
  • The numbers are the client's, not mine
  • Hours check passes, or the price was raised
  • A date is written down
  • The decision is made on the call, either way, without pressure
Done when

You can say your number, how it is built, and what you are doing next week.
