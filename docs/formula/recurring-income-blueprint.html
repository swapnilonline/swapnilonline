The road, in order

Skill → Offer → Client → Business → Scale

Five links. Each one ends with something you can hold, not something you learned. Most people skip from Skill to Scale and wonder why nothing earns. The line only rises when the links are built in order.

1SkillWhat people already pay you for, or ask you for. Not an idea. A thing you can do today.Done when: one real person has asked you for it in the last year.
2OfferThe result, for one kind of person, at a price, in a timeframe. Said in one sentence.Done when: a friend can repeat it back, price included.
3ClientSomeone has paid. From your own network first: one message to five people who know you.Done when: money has moved. Not agreed. Paid.
4BusinessThe page, the proposal, the calendar, the invoice. The second client arrives without starting from zero.Done when: a new enquiry goes from hello to paid on templates.
5ScaleRaise the price. Sell the next thing to existing clients. Automate the repeat. Then, a helper.Done when: income grew last quarter and your hours did not.
